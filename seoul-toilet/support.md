# Seoul Toilet Support

Seoul Toilet helps visitors find public restrooms in Seoul, with English addresses alongside the original Korean.

## Contact

Questions, problems, or wrong restroom information? Email us at <hello.braindance@gmail.com>.

If you are reporting a restroom, please include the name shown in the app (English and Korean) and what is wrong, for example "closed", "moved", or "wrong opening hours".

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to allow location access?

No. Without location access you can still move the map and view every restroom. If you allow it, the app shows where you are and the distance to nearby restrooms. Your location is used only on your device.

To change this, go to iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Seoul Toilet.

### The information for a restroom is wrong or out of date.

Restroom information comes from Korean public data, mainly the Seoul Metropolitan Government's public restroom data. Some details may be incomplete or out of date, so please check signs and access conditions on site. Let us know by email and we will review it in a future data update.

### "Get Directions" says it couldn't open NAVER Map.

The app opens walking directions in the NAVER Map app. If NAVER Map is not installed, you can open the location in Apple Maps instead, or install NAVER Map from the App Store.

### Can I use the app outside Seoul?

The app covers Seoul only. Outside Seoul you can still browse the map of Seoul, but nearby results and distances are not available.

### Why are there ads?

Seoul Toilet is free to use and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. The app does not ask for permission to track you across other apps and websites. See the Privacy Policy for details.
