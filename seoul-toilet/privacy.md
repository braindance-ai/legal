# Privacy Policy — Seoul Toilet

Effective date: [YYYY-MM-DD — set on release]

Seoul Toilet ("the app") is provided by braindance ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how. We have written it to match exactly what the app does.

## Summary

- You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
- Your location is used only on your device, only after you tap the location button, and only while the app is open.
- We do not run servers for this app, so we do not receive or store your location or any other personal information.
- The app has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

## 1. Location

When you tap the location button, the app asks for permission to use your location "while using the app". If you allow it, your location is used on your device to:

- show where you are on the map, and
- calculate the straight-line distance to nearby restrooms.

Your location is not sent to us, not saved on your device by the app, and not used in the background. If you do not allow location access, you can still move the map and view all restrooms; only your position and distances will not be shown.

You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Seoul Toilet.

## 2. Map service (NAVER Maps)

The map is provided by the NAVER Maps SDK (NAVER Cloud Platform). To display the map, your device downloads map data from NAVER's servers. As with any online map, NAVER may process technical information such as your IP address, device and app information, and the map areas being displayed. This is handled under NAVER's own privacy policy:
<https://policy.naver.com/policy/privacy_en.html>

We do not send your location to NAVER for any purpose of ours.

## 3. Directions

When you tap "Get Directions", the app opens the NAVER Map app (or Apple Maps if NAVER Map is not available) with the restroom's location as the destination. The app passes only the destination, not your current location. Once the other app opens, its own privacy policy applies.

## 4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or store: names, contact details, account information, photos, contacts, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, or crash reports.

## 5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## 6. Restroom information

Restroom locations, opening hours, and other details come from public data (see "Data sources" in the app). This information may be incomplete or out of date. Please check signs on site.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information differently, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.
